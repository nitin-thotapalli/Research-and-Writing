## Executive Summary & Preliminary Financial Assessment

**Preliminary conclusion:** Suzlon has substantially improved its profitability, operating scale and credit standing since its distressed years. FY2026 consolidated operating revenue grew approximately 54% to ₹16,679 crore, with EBITDA of ₹3,022 crore. Yet FY2026 cash from operating activities was only approximately ₹1,202 crore against reported PAT of ₹3,163 crore, and Q2 FY2027 EBITDA margin was below the prior-year comparable quarter. The balance sheet is significantly stronger than in the restructuring era, but cash conversion, project-linked working capital, tax-accounting effects and restricted cash warrant further diligence. [FY2026 results / F01](https://www.suzlon.com/pdf/green-business/Q4FY26_Results_Release_FINAL.pdf) · [FY2026 directors' financial summary / F02](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/suzlon-energy-ltd/reports/directors-report) · [Cash flow history / F03](https://www.screener.in/company/SUZLON/consolidated/) · [Q2 FY27 / F04](https://www.suzlon.com/media/press/press-releases/suzlon-q2-revenue-grows-26-yo-y-on-record-deliveries/)

- **Growth:** FY2026 revenue ₹16,679 crore versus ₹10,851 crore in FY2025; EBITDA ₹3,022 crore versus ₹1,857 crore.
- **Profit quality:** FY2026 PAT ₹3,163 crore includes significant tax effects; it must not be equated with recurring post-tax earning power.
- **Cash flow:** FY2026 CFO approximately ₹1,202 crore, capital expenditure approximately ₹577 crore, and simplified CFO minus capex approximately ₹625 crore.
- **Recent margins:** Q2 FY2027 revenue ₹4,869 crore, EBITDA ₹720 crore, EBITDA margin 14.8%, compared with 18.6% a year earlier.
- **Credit:** CRISIL upgraded long-term bank-facility rating to AA-/Stable on 27 October 2026, while explicitly highlighting the historic defaults and working-capital intensity. [CRISIL / F05](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SuzlonEnergyLimited_October%2027_%202026_RR_406078.html)

## 4. Earnings Quality & Normalization

### 4.1 Reported earnings are not necessarily recurring cash earnings

Q2 FY2026's reported PAT includes a ₹891 crore deferred tax asset recognition effect. Suzlon's October 2026 release explicitly provides a comparable Q2 FY2026 PAT of ₹389 crore versus the reported ₹1,279 crore. [F04](https://www.suzlon.com/media/press/press-releases/suzlon-q2-revenue-grows-26-yo-y-on-record-deliveries/)

**Adjustment principles:**

- Identify non-cash tax gains/charges, exceptional items, asset disposals, reversals and restructuring effects.
- Show reported PAT, normalized pre-tax earnings and normalized cash tax separately.
- Do not automatically exclude every item labelled exceptional; assess recurrence and economic meaning.
- Do not assume a perpetual zero/negative effective tax rate when forecasting.
- Keep a reconciliation from reported financials to adjusted financials with a filing-note reference.

### 4.2 Normalization workpaper

| Adjustment | FY2026 impact | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deferred tax recognition / tax credits | Material; quantify from tax note | Annual tax note and quarter disclosures |
| Exceptional income / expense | Requires reconciliation | Annual exceptional-items disclosure |
| Acquisition / amalgamation effects | Potentially material | Subsidiary financials and consolidation note |
| One-time gains / asset sales | Historical relevance | Statement of profit and loss; other income note |
| Normalized cash tax | Model assumption, not a disclosed figure | Tax schedule and eligible losses |

### 5.1 Working-capital bridge to build

| Item | Financial meaning | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trade receivables | Sales not yet collected | DSO; customer concentration and ageing |
| Contract assets / unbilled revenue | Work recognised before unconditional billing rights | Acceptance milestones and billing delays |
| Inventory and work in progress | Cash tied in manufacturing and project execution | DIO; spares vs turbine assemblies |
| Trade payables | Supplier financing | DPO; sustainability of terms |
| Customer advances / contract liabilities | Cash received before corresponding delivery | Order quality and potential refund obligations |
| Guarantees and restricted deposits | Cash or credit capacity encumbered for contracts | Available versus reported liquidity |

Build DSO, DIO and DPO only after verifying consistent opening/closing balances, revenue/COGS denominators, and construction-contract accounting. Avoid interpreting a cash build-up as unrestricted liquidity.

## 6. Liquidity, Capital Structure & Credit Quality

CRISIL upgraded the bank loan ratings to **AA-/Stable and A1+** in October 2026. CRISIL said total cash and equivalents were approximately ₹2,599 crore as of June 30, 2026, including around ₹1,392 crore free cash, with the remaining amount encumbered. This is not equivalent to all cash being freely deployable for shareholders. [CRISIL, 27 October 2026 / F05](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SuzlonEnergyLimited_October%2027_%202026_RR_406078.html)

Suzlon separately reported a **₹2,384 crore net-cash position at March 31, 2026**. Do not directly subtract or compare the March “net cash” number and the June CRISIL gross-cash/free-cash breakdown without reconciling their definitions, debt treatment and dates. [FY2026 release / F01](https://www.suzlon.com/pdf/green-business/Q4FY26_Results_Release_FINAL.pdf)

| Indicator | Position / evidence | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rating | CRISIL AA-/Stable long-term; A1+ short-term | Bank-facility credit quality, not an equity buy signal |
| March 2026 management “net cash” | ₹2,384 cr | Definition, restrictions and deposits need reconciliation |
| June 2026 cash and equivalents per CRISIL | ₹2,599 cr | ₹1,392 cr free cash; remainder encumbered |
| Debt maturities | CRISIL described scheduled principal repayments as modest | Guarantees, lease obligations and non-fund credit still matter |
| Non-fund working-capital usage | CRISIL described 79% average limit utilisation over 12 months ended July 2026 | Could limit incremental project execution flexibility |
| Financial history | Defaults and a 2020 restructuring | Historic stress requires stress testing, not assumptions of permanent fragility |

### Analytical conclusion

Leverage and credit access have improved substantially. The key financial question has shifted from immediate balance-sheet survival to **how growth consumes working capital and whether capital allocation sustains cash returns**.

## 7. Profitability, Leverage & Return Metrics

| Framework | Calculation | What it reveals | Current assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue growth | Revenue(t) / Revenue(t−1) − 1 | Growth | FY2026 ≈53.7% |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA / revenue | Operating economics before D&A | FY2026 ≈18.1%; Q2 FY2027 14.8% |
| EBIT margin | EBIT / revenue | After depreciation | Calculate after harmonising EBIT definition |
| CFO conversion | CFO / EBITDA and CFO / normalized PAT | Cash earnings quality | FY2026 CFO / EBITDA ≈39.8% |
| Net debt / EBITDA | (Interest-bearing debt − accessible cash) / EBITDA | Financial leverage | Requires an explicitly defined usable-cash figure |
| Current ratio | Current assets / current liabilities | Short-term coverage | Confirm from Sep 2026 statutory balance sheet |
| DSO / DIO / DPO | Days-based working-capital components | Operating cash cycle | Complete after notes reconciliation |
| ROCE | EBIT / average capital employed | Returns on operating capital | Calculate on adjusted consistent basis |
| ROIC vs WACC | NOPAT / average invested capital vs cost of capital | Economic value creation | Requires normalized taxes and capital employed |
| DuPont ROE | Net margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier | ROE drivers | Beware tax gains and historic changes in equity |

**Do not promote unadjusted ROE as proof of quality:** Deferred tax, exceptional gains and equity recapitalisation can temporarily inflate it.

## 9. Segment Economics & Peer Financial Benchmarking

CRISIL states that the wind-turbine-generator (WTG) business contributed about **67% of consolidated EBITDA in FY2026**. Service earnings can be structurally different from turbine/project earnings; model them separately if segment disclosures permit. [F05](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SuzlonEnergyLimited_October%2027_%202026_RR_406078.html)

| Economic segment | Financial questions | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turbine equipment | ASP / MW, gross margin, raw materials, fixed-cost absorption | Extract segment revenue and earnings |
| EPC / project execution | Contract margin, milestone timing, advance funding, warranty and overruns | Separate EPC scope and segment reporting |
| O&M / services | Recurrence, renewal, contract margins, service capex | Verify revenue and sustainable profit |
| Developing integrated offerings | Mix change, investment requirements, reported economics | Do not value strategic targets as realised revenue |

**Peer discipline:** Use Inox Wind or other comparable OEMs with aligned reporting periods and revenue mix; compare IPPs only as industry-adjacent economics, not direct turbine-manufacturer financial comps.

## 11. Financial Model Architecture & Validation Controls

Build an Excel / Google Sheets workbook with the following linked tabs:

1. Sources & provenance: document, link, date, reporting scope and units.
2. Historical P&L: FY2017–FY2026 plus separate interim quarters.
3. Historical balance sheet: cash, debt, operating assets and liabilities.
4. Historical cash flow: CFO, investing, financing, capex, reconciliation.

6. Operating drivers: orders → MW delivered → commission / acceptance → revenue.
7. Working capital and liquidity: DSO, inventory, advances, restricted cash.
8. Ratio dashboard: growth, margins, credit, ROIC/ROCE, DuPont.
9. Review checks: balance-sheet equality, cash bridge, growth rates, units, restatements.
10. Scenario inputs to feed Level 4 DCF and Level 5 risk model.

**Model rules:** Blue/font or consistent input convention for assumptions; distinguish hardcoded historical values from forecasts; never overwrite primary-source figures with adjusted values; show every adjustment and source note.

## 14. Investment Research Handoff

**Established:** Strong historical revenue growth, improved financial position, weak cash-profit conversion relative to reported PAT, and recent EBITDA-margin pressure.

**Unresolved:** Sustainable margins, normalized tax burden, unrestricted cash, working-capital needs and true incremental ROIC.

**For Level 4:** Forecast recurring operating profit and free cash flow—not tax-distorted earnings—and identify a defensible capital structure/enterprise-to-equity bridge.

**For Level 5:** Stress cash collection, deliveries, margin, project execution and guarantees; test whether liquidity remains sufficient through a downcycle.
