## 1. How to Use the Question Bank

- **High-level (H):** Questions an investment committee or portfolio manager must answer to deploy capital or reject the idea.
- **Mid-level (M):** Analytical questions that reveal business, financial and competitive mechanisms behind the thesis.
- **Low-level (L):** Evidence and data verification questions that prevent errors, weak assumptions and misleading comparisons.
- **Pressure tests (PT):** Deliberately adversarial scenarios designed to disprove or materially weaken a preferred thesis.

**Workflow:** Pick a question → write a hypothesis → locate primary evidence → calculate or compare → seek counterevidence → document answer and confidence → update the relevant research stage → revisit the decision.

**Answer statuses:** Open · Researching · Evidence incomplete · Answered · Contradicted · Not publicly verifiable.

**Suggested priority:** P0 = could change invest/avoid decision; P1 = material model or operating insight; P2 = supporting context. Start with falsifiers and cash economics before expanding the question list further.

## 5. Adversarial IC Pressure Tests (12)

The following are **challenges to the thesis**. Do not treat the scenario premises as reported facts.

| Test | Portfolio manager's challenge | Failure mode / analytical response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PT01** | Remove the India 100 GW ambition from the thesis. Is Suzlon still attractive using only contracted project economics? | If the case collapses, it is policy-target-dependent. |
| **PT02** | Assume 25% of the reported backlog slips by two years. Which cash milestones and valuation assumptions break first? | Test timing, deposits, inventory, financing and cash conversion. |
| **PT03** | Suppose delivery MW grows 30% but EBITDA remains flat. Would you still call this value-creating growth? | Test incremental EBIT and ROIC, not just revenue. |
| **PT04** | Could an apparently large service fleet have low retention, warranty-heavy costs or contracts priced too cheaply? | Demand evidence of renewals, margins and service cash flow. |
| **PT05** | What if the 6+ GW order book is concentrated in three customers or dependent on their financing? | Test customer concentration and cancellations, not just aggregate GW. |
| **PT06** | How does the investment conclusion change if all non-cash tax benefits are excluded? | Compare reported and normalized PAT, cash taxes and FCFF. |
| **PT07** | What if 30–60 additional receivable days appear during a high-growth year? | Stress working capital and unrestricted liquidity before celebrating margins. |
| **PT08** | Can an alternative OEM deliver similar reliability at lower customer lifetime cost? | Use independent total-cost-of-energy comparisons and pricing power evidence. |
| **PT09** | Would the thesis survive if management missed two consecutive delivery or commissioning milestones? | Specify operational falsifiers, not retrospective justifications. |
| **PT10** | If the share price were unchanged but the DCF terminal value accounted for most of EV, would you approve? | Challenge growth horizon, reinvestment, WACC and exit assumptions. |
| **PT11** | Is the turnaround story masking shareholder dilution or value transferred to creditors? | Compare enterprise survival, shareholder per-share return and financing terms. |
| **PT12** | Could a wrong conclusion arise simply from mixing MW delivered, MW commissioned, serviced MW and backlog? | Recreate every numerator and denominator from cited dates and definitions. |

## 8. Investment Committee: Questions That Must Be Answered Before Approval

- [ ] Is industry growth translating into good OEM economics, not only increased MW?
- [ ] Are Suzlon's stated advantages independently measurable and sustainable?
- [ ] Are the order book and project execution pipeline reliable?
- [ ] Can we explain normalized profits and their conversion into free cash flow?
- [ ] Do we know unrestricted liquidity, contingent commitments and downside funding needs?
- [ ] Do we have a checked intrinsic-value range and market-implied expectations?
- [ ] Have we tested the strongest credible argument against our thesis?
- [ ] Do we have explicit conditions to Invest, Wait, Avoid or Investigate further?
- [ ] Can the recommendation be explained in two minutes with sources, assumptions and the single largest uncertainty?
