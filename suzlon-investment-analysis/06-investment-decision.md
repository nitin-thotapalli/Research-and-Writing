## 2. Executive Investment Thesis

**Draft thesis:** Suzlon may benefit from a renewed Indian wind-energy deployment cycle because it combines domestic manufacturing, a substantial installed service network, a sizeable reported order book and increasingly integrated project offerings. Yet business scale and contractual pipeline alone are insufficient to justify a valuation premium. Shareholder returns depend on whether orders are executed profitably, working-capital investment is recovered, cash earnings normalize and return on capital exceeds the cost of capital.

**What could be underappreciated:** Long-term service economics and better execution cadence could make the business less cyclical than a pure turbine supplier—if independently supported by segment and cash-flow evidence.

**What could be overlooked:** Non-cash tax effects, working-capital intensity, restricted liquidity, EPC execution risks and lower recent EBITDA margins.

**Key falsifiable question:** Does operating growth consistently produce higher *unrestricted free cash flow per diluted share*, after investment in production and customer projects?

## 4. Investment Merits — Evidence-Supported Arguments

### 4.1 Structural industry opportunity

Indian installed wind capacity and FY2026 installations increased materially, with a policy ambition to grow wind to 100 GW by 2030. This expands the potential market but does not guarantee company order wins or profitable project delivery. [01. Industry Analysis](01-industry-analysis.md)

### 4.2 Commercial scale and order visibility

Suzlon reported 6+ GW of orders in October 2026 and 710 MW Q2 FY2027 deliveries; its 16+ GW serviced India wind fleet could support recurring relationships. These are different operating metrics and must not be simply summed or assumed to become revenue immediately. [IC03](https://www.suzlon.com/media/press/press-releases/suzlon-q2-revenue-grows-26-yo-y-on-record-deliveries/)

### 4.3 Financial restructuring and credit improvement

Past defaults, restructuring and refinancing reduced debt stress; CRISIL upgraded the company's long-term bank credit rating to AA-/Stable in October 2026. Creditworthiness has improved but does not directly establish equity undervaluation. [IC05](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SuzlonEnergyLimited_October%2027_%202026_RR_406078.html)

### 4.4 Potential integrated-platform advantage

The company's technology, DevCo, EPC and asset-management architecture may create customer convenience and capture more of the project life cycle. Treat future FY2031 strategic ambitions as targets until converted to measurable execution and earnings. [02 — Business Analysis](02-business-analysis.md)

## 5. Key Counterarguments & Investment Risks

- **Margin sustainability:** Q2 FY2027 EBITDA margin was 14.8% versus 18.6% in Q2 FY2026, despite revenue growth. [IC03](https://www.suzlon.com/media/press/press-releases/suzlon-q2-revenue-grows-26-yo-y-on-record-deliveries/)
- **Earnings versus cash:** FY2026 PAT was far higher than CFO. Deferred tax, receivables, inventory and project assets need independent reconciliation. [IC02](https://www.suzlon.com/pdf/green-business/Q4FY26_Results_Release_FINAL.pdf) · [IC04](https://www.screener.in/company/SUZLON/consolidated/)
- **Liquidity restrictions:** CRISIL's June 2026 gross cash estimate included encumbered balances; do not assume all reported cash is available to shareholders. [IC05](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SuzlonEnergyLimited_October%2027_%202026_RR_406078.html)
- **Orders versus execution:** EPC and project readiness can create schedule and cost risk even after equipment is delivered. [02 — Business Analysis](02-business-analysis.md)
- **Competition:** Domestic and foreign OEMs contest new wind installations; large historic installed-base share does not guarantee current commissioning share. [01. Industry Analysis](01-industry-analysis.md)
- **Valuation uncertainty:** Without normalized FCFF and an independently justified WACC, the current market price cannot be classified as attractive or unattractive with discipline. [04 — Valuation](04-valuation.md)

## 8. Dissenting View & Investment Committee Challenge

**Bull case to challenge:** India's wind cycle and Suzlon's integrated model will drive sustained higher delivery, pricing power and service income.

**Bear case to challenge:** Wind orders grow faster than commissioning and cash collection; more EPC exposure and competition dilute margins while valuation overstates sustainable FCF.

**Unresolved committee questions:**

1. What share of the 6+ GW backlog is firm, financed and execution-ready?
2. Does turbine/EPC mix generate reliable profit and acceptable cash conversion?
3. How much unrestricted cash remains after commitments and guarantees?
4. What proportion of reported PAT is non-recurring or tax-accounting-driven?
5. Can service earnings grow without disproportionate maintenance liabilities?
6. What DCF assumptions are embedded in the 4 Nov 2026 trading price?
7. Under which scenario would existing shareholders suffer permanent value impairment?
8. Which public fact would cause us to abandon the thesis?
