## Scope and evidence

The articles distinguish independent analytical exercises and synthetic calculations from customer-validated or deployed product outcomes. Relevant public references appear within the original case studies. Some source documents discuss a particular worked example rather than every aspect of the broader problem; the original problem statements are included above each detailed study to preserve that distinction.
