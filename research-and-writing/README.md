# Research & Writing

> **Space for exploring ideas.**

This section will hold independent research, essays, technical notes and longer-form writing about finance, product management, analytics and AI.

Unlike [Case Studies](../case-studies/README.md), which begin with a specific problem and work toward a decision, pieces here can begin with a question, observation, hypothesis or idea.

Long, independent research projects (for example, full company investment analyses supported by notebooks and models) can live in their **own GitHub repositories**. This folder is for shorter pieces and broader thinking.

There are no published articles in this folder yet.
