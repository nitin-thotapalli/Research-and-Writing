## How to read this work

The articles follow a consistent, client-oriented structure: **the ask → worked results → potential business impact → diagnosis → proposed solution → validation plan → recommendation**.

The numerical scenarios are identified as synthetic where applicable. Publicly documented examples are cited in the relevant articles. A correctly solved example is **not** presented as measured product deployment, client benefit or guaranteed financial impact.

For more extended, end-to-end product problem-solving, see [Case Studies](../case-studies/README.md). Larger investment research, models and technical projects may live in separate repositories.
